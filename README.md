# Walter's Adventures

A side-scrolling swim-and-survive game about **Walter**, who starts life looking just like an axolotl and grows up into a **Western Tiger Salamander**.

**Play it:** https://markwarddesign.github.io/walters-adventure/

Walter is based on a real Western Tiger Salamander who was a much-loved family pet.

## How to play

- **Swim / walk:** arrow keys or WASD — or touch/click and hold where you want Walter to go
- **Dash:** Space (or the DASH button on touch screens)
- **Pause:** P or Esc · **Sound:** M

Eat food to fill the **Growth** meter, then head to the exit on the far right. Hide in weeds, rocks, logs, ferns and burrows when predators come near. Lost a heart? Wait a bit — salamanders regenerate!

## Levels

| # | Level | Walter is a… | Eats | Watch out for |
|---|-------|--------------|------|---------------|
| 1 | The Shallows | Hatchling | daphnia, mosquito larvae | dragonfly nymphs, diving beetles |
| 2 | Lily Pad Lane | Little Larva | bloodworms, mosquito larvae | sunfish, beetles, fishing hooks |
| 3 | The Deep Pond | Big Larva | minnows, worms | bass, snapping turtles, a heron |
| 4 | Changing Times | Morphing Juvenile (needs air!) | minnows, worms | bullfrogs, bass, heron |
| 5 | The Forest Floor | Tiger Salamander | crickets, beetles, earthworms | garter snakes, raccoon, owl |

Bonus rounds between levels: **Bubble Bonanza**, **Axolotl Quiz**, **Morph Match**, **Rainy Night Feast** and **The Big Quiz**.

## Learning

36 real axolotl and tiger salamander facts show up in fact bubbles, level cards and quizzes. Discovered facts are saved in **Walter's Fact Journal** on the title screen.

## Tech

One self-contained `index.html` (HTML5 canvas + Web Audio, no build step). Served by GitHub Pages from `main`.
